Welcome to Changes 1.0

Changes is a playable jazz harmony instrument: one key per chord in your left hand, an always-in-key melody in your right, and music theory from beginner to conservatory level.

This installer adds:

  • the Changes app in your Applications folder
  • the Audio Unit plug-in for Logic Pro, GarageBand and MainStage
  • the VST3 plug-in for Ableton Live, Cubase, Reaper, Studio One, Bitwig and FL Studio
  • the manual and an uninstaller

Runs natively on Apple Silicon and Intel Macs, macOS 10.13 or later. Click Customize on the Installation Type page to choose components.
